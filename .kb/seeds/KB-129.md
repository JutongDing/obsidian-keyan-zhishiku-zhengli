published articles and their growing citation counts [5].

## 结论节选
In conclusion, this study introduces a novel adaptive control framework for robotic grippers designed to handle a diverse range
of compliant objects by emulating human grasping behaviours. By integrating classical PID control, PI-FLC, and RL, the proposed
system achieves precise and reliable force modulation during object manipulation. Experimental validation using a two-ﬁnger gripper
prototype demonstrated the framework’s eﬀectiveness across various stiﬀness levels, including rigid (iron, plastic) and deformable
(silicone, foam, sponge) materials.
 The results indicate that while PID control performs adequately with rigid objects, it struggles to accommodate the nonlinear
dynamics of softer materials. Conversely, the PI-Fuzzy and RL controllers demonstrate signiﬁcantly improved force tracking accu-
racy, stability, and generalisation, closely resembling human-like grasping patterns. Notably, the PI-Fuzzy controller exhibits superior
adaptability through rule-based modulation, while RL excels in autonomously learning optimal control strategies for varying compli-
ance levels.
 These ﬁndings, supported by statistical analyses (t-tests) and frequency domain evaluations, highlight the potential for combin-
ing classical and intelligent control strategies to enhance robotic dexterity
