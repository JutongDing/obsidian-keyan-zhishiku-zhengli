 during tool using, as well as the initial grasping locations. Accordingly, we sample a series of grasps and score them to identify stable
 grasp candidates (middle left). A reinforcement learning policy then performs online-grasp adaptation by adjusting finger joint positions
 under task-specific force conditioning (middle right). The resulting policy is zero-shot deployed in the real world (right).
