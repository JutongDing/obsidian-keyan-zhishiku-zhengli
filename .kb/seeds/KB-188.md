## 摘要（英文）
Abstract ：The peel of Citrus reticulata fruits is relatively fragile. In the planting and post-harvest processing stages, the picking oper‐
ation of Citrus reticulata fruits mainly relies on manual labor, which leads to a large demand for labor and easily causes damage to
the fruits. The existing spherical fruit picking mechanical claws have problems such as poor universality and are difficult to meet the
actual needs. A dexterous hand for grasping and fixing Citrus reticulata fruits was designed, and a three-dimensional model was con‐
ducted. The design of the power system and control system was introduced. A prototype and experimental platform were built, and
fingertip output force and grasping tests were carried out. The test results showed that the maximum fingertip output force of the de‐
signed dexteric hand could reach 12.42 N, which could grasp Citrus reticulata fruits with diameters ranging from 40 to 80 mm, veri‐
fying its feasibility in the application of Citrus reticulata grasping and fixation.

## 摘要（中文）
柑橘果皮较为脆弱，在种植和采后加工等环节，柑橘的抓取操作主要依赖人工，导致劳动力需求量大且容易造成果实
的损坏，现有的球状水果抓取机械爪存在通用性差等问题，难以满足实际需求。基于此，设计了一种用于柑橘抓取固定的灵巧
手。通过三维建模，搭建样机和试验平台，进行指尖输出力和抓取测试，结果表明，设计的灵巧手最大指尖输出力达 12.42 N，
可以抓取果径在 40~80 mm 的柑橘果实，验证了其在柑橘抓取固定应用中的可行性。
