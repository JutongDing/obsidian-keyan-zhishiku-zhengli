## 摘要（英文）
We introduce and evaluate contact-based tech-
niques to estimate tactile properties and detect manipulation
events using a biomimetic tactile sensor. In particular, we
estimate finger forces, and detect and classify slip events. In
addition, we present a grip force controller that uses the
estimation results to gently pick up objects of various weights
and texture. The estimation techniques and the grip controller
are experimentally evaluated on a robotic system consisting of
Barrett arms and hands. Our results indicate that we are able
to accurately estimate forces acting in all directions, detect the
incipient slip, and classify slip with over 80% success rate.
