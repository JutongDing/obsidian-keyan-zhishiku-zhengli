## 摘要（英文）
Purpose – In space robot assembly scenarios, the microgravity environment causes target components to move randomly, making traditional peg-
 in-hole assembly methods in static scenario ineffective. The purpose of this paper is to develop a robot assisted peg-in-hole assembly strategy for
 holes in random motion, which is a primary step for in-space assembly.
 Design/methodology/approach – The proposed 3D tactile servo control based dynamic hole assembly strategy includes three main parts. First, by
 using the in-hand tactile information of the grasped peg, i.e. curl and the center of pressure, a tactile servo controller is developed for the real-time
 tracking of the random motion of the hole platform. Then, a hole searching method which includes active contact exploration and the exponentially
 growing sinusoidal search trajectory is designed toward dynamic hole searching. Lastly, they design extensive experiments and ablation studies to
 verify the effectiveness of the proposed methods.
 Findings – Compared to the baseline method, the proposed dynamic peg-in-hole assembly strategy significantly enhances the task success rate and
 efficiency. Our 3D tactile based method achieves a 90% success rate in 30 trials, compared to 73.33% for the baseline. Meanwhile, the task
 consuming-time is 26.5% decrease.
 Practical implications – The strategy can be used for industrial robot assembly with the moving production line.

 Abstract
 Purpose – In space robot assembly scenarios, the microgravity environment causes target components to move randomly, making traditional peg-
 in-hole assembly methods in static scenario ineffective. The purpose of this paper is to develop a robot assisted peg-in-hole assembly strategy for
 holes in random motion, which is a primary step for in-space assembly.
 Design/methodology/approach – The proposed 3D tactile servo control based dynamic hole assembly strategy includes three main parts. First, by
 using the in-hand tactile information of the grasped peg, i.e. curl and the center of pressure, a tactile servo controller is developed for the real-time
 tracking of the random motion of the hole platform. Then, a hole searching method which includes active contact exploration and the exponentially
 growing sinusoidal search trajectory is designed toward dynamic hole searching. Lastly, they design extensive experiments and ablation studies to
 verify the effectiveness of the proposed methods.
 Findings – Compared to the baseline method, the proposed dynamic peg-in-hole assembly strategy significantly enhances the task success rate and
 efficiency. Our 3D tactile based method achieves a 90% success rate in 30 trials, compared to 73.33% for the baseline. Meanwhile, the task
 consuming-time is 26.5% decrease.
 Practical implications – The strategy can be used for industrial robot assembly with the moving production line.
 Originality/value – A 3D tactile servo based assembly strategy is proposed to realize the dynamic peg-in-hole assembly tasks.
