Abstract

on areas such as service robotics, manufacturing, and logistics. Despite notable progress
in specific scenarios in recent years, existing grasping technologies still lack universally
applicable solutions, failing to meet the industry's demands for rapid design and
convenient deployment. In general grasping tasks, real-time perception plays a pivotal
role in determining grasp success and stability. One of the main drivers of robotic grasping

compliant grasping strategies based on a multimodal perception dexterous hand platform.
