 Abstract—In the field of grasping strategies for under-driven
 dexterous hands, intricate dynamic modeling is crucial to build
 the relationship between motor input currents and fingertip
 output forces. Compared with previous study, this study
 simplified the dynamics modeling, which only needs to build
 kinematics model and creates the grasping strategy based on
 geometric structure of the object. Future work envisions shape-
 based object classification, resulting in customized gripping
 strategies and a library of geometry-specific gripping methods.

 Keywords-component; under-driven dexterous hand, humanoid
 robot, grasping strategy.
