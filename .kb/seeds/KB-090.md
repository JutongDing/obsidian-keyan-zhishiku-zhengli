 Abhinav Grover∗ , Philippe Nadeau∗ , Christopher Grebe, and Jonathan Kelly†


 Abstract— The ability to perceive object slip via tactile
 feedback enables humans to accomplish complex manipulation
 tasks including maintaining a stable grasp. Despite the utility of
 tactile information for many applications, tactile sensors have
 yet to be widely deployed in industrial robotics settings; part
 of the challenge lies in identifying slip and other events from

 These sensors have many desirable properties including high
 durability and reliability, and are built from inexpensive, off-
